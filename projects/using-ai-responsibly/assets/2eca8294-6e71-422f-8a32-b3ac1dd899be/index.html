Using Copilot for Workplace Writing

Marcus, a nursing faculty member, needs to document a student concern. Follow his process as he prepares information for Copilot and decides what to do with the draft it creates.

Marcus has noticed several concerns involving Alexis, one of his students. She has missed two required clinical days this term, previously reported transportation difficulties, and is requesting a make-up for the most recent missed clinical day. Her academic performance has also declined, including a 68% on her most recent exam and three missing case study assignments.

Marcus reviews the Academic Risk Awareness Playbook in the Faculty Toolkit. Based on the pattern he has observed, he determines that additional outreach and support are appropriate and uses the Student Outreach and Support Form to document his concerns and request support for Alexis.

Review Marcus's notes carefully. You will refer to this information later in the scenario.
